# The stone bridge (2026-11-18)

Made on the owner's paid Meshy account (no credit needed in the game). The community library was not searched (it needs the owner's signed-in Chrome, and the bridge needed a known size and shape).

- Picture: Meshy text-to-image, `nano-banana-pro`, 16:9, 9 credits, task `01a1eee5-657f-7049-8297-b8d001119da8` (`StoneBridge_picture.png`). A first picture (task `01a1eee3-b2ee-7592-ba7e-d1a981c2a683`, 9 credits) had no near parapet, a sandy colour and a narrow deck, so it was redrawn: side-on, both parapets, wide deck, cool grey stone with a few tan stones, plain blue-grey backdrop (no background removal).
  Prompt (second): a broad medieval stone arch bridge with three round arches, seen from the side slightly above, BOTH parapet walls clearly visible with a squat round post at each end, a wide cobbled deck about five metres across, gently humped, short ramps with flat ends, piers with pointed cutwaters, cut off flat at the bottom; "Stylised hand-painted game asset, Breath of the Wild meets Overwatch's medieval towns: simple but smooth and detailed, not realistic, a tiny bit cartoonish, HD RuneScape feel, crisp bevels, rich clean colours, cool mid-grey stone blocks with a few warm tan stones, no dirt, no rust, no moss"; no water, ground, trees, people, lanterns, banners or signs.
- Model: Meshy image-to-3d from that picture, `meshy-7.1`, standard, textured, PBR, image enhancement off, GLB, 30 credits, task `01a1eee6-1119-774f-b2fc-120ae7fd6384`. `StoneBridge_Detailed.glb` (24 MB, 552k triangles, git-ignored: fetch it again from the task).
- Spent: 48 credits.
- Game version: `Tools/BuildBridge.ps1` (`bridge_clean.py` in Blender: welded, reduced to 280k triangles, 1400 cm per model unit, origin at the span's middle on the pier bottoms, stone warmed a little; pictures 2048/2048/1024; `bridge_import.py` in Unreal: `/Game/Bridge`, Nanite, own plain material `M_Bridge`). Measured profile of the model: `Tools/bridge_profile.py` (the deck and underside heights are tabled in `Source/Jeebsland/Bridge.cpp`; re-measure and update them if the model is ever replaced).
