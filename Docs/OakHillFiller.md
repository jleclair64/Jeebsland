# Oak Hill filler props (owner, 2026-11-12)

Small models copied many times to fill Oak Hill's empty space, cheap on storage (one model, many copies). Find them in Meshy's free library first (Docs/MeshyCommunityLibrary.md), reduce each to a few thousand triangles, and place them as separate decorations (never part of a building model).

Already in the game, code-built: hay bales, pumpkins, lamp posts, scarecrows, village fences.

**Start with:** barrels, crates, sacks, hay bales, stone walls, a cart, benches, lamp posts, boulders, bushes.

- **Storage and goods:** barrels (single, stacked, tipped), crates (open with goods, closed, stacked), grain and flour sacks, woodpiles and log stacks, rope coils and nets, clay pots and jars, wicker baskets.
- **Farm and yard:** round and square hay bales, a small haystack, carts, wheelbarrows, water troughs, buckets, chicken coops, dog kennels, garden beds, cabbage rows, a chopping block with an axe.
- **Walls and borders:** low dry-stone walls (straight, corner, broken end), wooden fences and gates, hedges, stone steps on the hills.
- **Bushes (owner asked for these specifically):** several sizes of round leafy bushes, flowering bushes, low shrubs along walls and house fronts, bushes at road edges and corners.
- **Street dressing:** benches and picnic tables, lamp posts and post lanterns (can carry real light), signposts and notice boards, market umbrellas, awnings and banners on poles, flower boxes and potted plants by doors, small wells and pumps.
- **Nature fill:** boulders and mossy rocks, ferns and wildflower patches, tree stumps and fallen logs, puddles and mud patches on the roads.
