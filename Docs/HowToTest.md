# How to test Jeebsland

**Rule of thumb: the editor's Play button never needs the worlds. The .bat files are the real thing.**

| I want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Quickly try a change, alone | Editor: green Play ▶ (any mode). Net Mode "Play Offline". |
| Try it online, quickly (2 players, no setup) | Editor: ⋮ next to Play > Net Mode "Play As Client", Number of Players 2 > ▶. Logs in as Tester 1 / Tester 2. |
| The full game: starting screen, accounts, both worlds | Close the editor. `Tools\HostWorlds.bat`, wait for "Jeebsland world up", then `Tools\RunClient.bat` (twice for 2 players). Address `127.0.0.1`. Account names use only letters, numbers and spaces. |
| See exactly what friends get | Same as above, but open `Packaged\Windows\Jeebsland.exe` instead of RunClient (only right after repackaging). |

In the editor's preview window (New Editor Window / Selected Viewport), Escape stops Play and it can't go full screen: use **O**
for the map / settings screen there. For full screen with a working Escape, pick **Standalone Game** (⋮ next to Play > Modes):
it plays offline straight away. (Alt is the roll key, so Alt+Enter just rolls.) To change the editor's Stop key: Editor
Preferences > Keyboard Shortcuts > search "Stop" > set it to Shift+Escape.

## Oak Hill (2026-11-11)

Field map, Play. Oak Hill is about 185 m east of the start, across the river (there is no bridge yet, so wade across where its west road stops at the bank). The world map (O) shows its name and its streets. Head for them and look for the big oak on the plaza.
- What's there: the plaza with its big oak and the well, the smith, bank, store and potion shop round the ring road, the town hall to the east, the church on its rise to the northeast, and 17 houses (two designs). The streets follow the approved plan. The ground has its own gentle hills: the plaza is just over 6 m above the river and the castle knoll about 9 m. There's grass and there are trees.
- Left empty on purpose, for what isn't made yet: the boxing ring (south of the plaza, levelled), the castle ruins (on the knoll to the north), the garden behind the town hall, the graveyard (southeast), both forts and the watchtower by the river, and the bridge.
- Test pictures from the air: `-ShotEye=X_Y_Z` puts a free camera there, looking along `-ShotYaw` and `-ShotPitch` (`-ShotFov` too). Add `-ShotAt=X,Y` to stand the knight there, so the grass grows round him. `-NoOakHill` (test pictures only) leaves the town out, to compare frame rates; `-NoOakGrass`, `-NoOakMeshes` (also the stones and shrubs at its walls) and `-NoOakTrees` leave out just that part, and `-NoWorldGrass` the meadow grass outside the start's circle and the town (it grows over all the world's grassy ground since 2026-11-12). The plaza is at X -3550, Y 18290; a view of the whole town from the air: `-ShotEye=-9550_13290_6500 -ShotYaw=40 -ShotPitch=-37`. The log's `JEEBOAK` lines give the heights, each road's steepest grade, where each building went, and the closest two buildings.

## Chat and the radar potion (2026-10-30)

- **Chat:** press **Enter**, type, Enter again to send (Escape cancels). Players within 35 m hear it and see it over your head. `/w message` tells the whole world. `/mute Name`, `/unmute Name`, `/mutelist` hide players on your machine. Bad words turn into asterisks (the server does it). Try it with "Play As Client", 2 players.
- **Owner tools:** make `Saved/Moderation.txt` on the server's machine with lines like `mute Sir Jeeb` (they can play but nobody hears them) or `ban Sir Jeeb` (cannot log in). It is re-read every few seconds, no restart. Lines starting with `#` are comments.
- **Radar potion:** the practice pack has three (the store sells it for 60 ingots). Put it in the potion slot (drag it there) and press Q: enemy knights and other players near you show as red dots on the minimap for 2 minutes. Ready again after 3 minutes.

## MoveLab: a small test area for moves (2026-10-31)

For working on attacks and movement: in the editor's Content Browser double-click **MoveLab**, then press Play. It loads in seconds: a flat floor with a line every metre (a brighter one every 5 m) a still sparring knight 3 m ahead (he never fights back or falls; his health fills again 3 s after your last hit), a second one 3.5 m to his right (as you look from the start) who always holds his shield up, and a straw dummy 3 m to each side. Both knights face where you start, so walk round them to try other angles. Hit boxes and hurt boxes show there, Smash style: yellow capsules are where a body can be hit, red spheres are the sword's hit boxes during the frames a swing can land (each drawn from where it was the frame before). Press **H** to hide or show them (in the real game they start hidden; H shows them there too). It is the same knight as in the game, so anything tuned here is already in the real game. To go back, double-click **Field**.

**The knight (2026-11-05):** there is only one knight now, for you and for everyone else: no N key and no old knight to go back to. Everything plays from animation clips with all your normal keys: walk, run, sidestep, back up, sprint, jump, roll (double-tap), crouch, raise the shield, the two-hit sword jab, the held smash, the kick (wheel up), the low sweep (wheel down), the kick into the sweep, the dash attack, and the bow. The sparring knights take the hits, and H shows the boxes: yellow hurt boxes on the body, red hit boxes on the weapon or the kicking leg, and the blue shield box where the raised shield is. A move's clip and timing can be changed in `Content/Data/KnightMoves.csv` with no build.

**Climbing and the mantle (2026-11-12):** in MoveLab, about 11 m ahead of the start and a little to the left (in front of the pond) stands a grey rock 5 m high with a grassy top. Walk into its sheer face (the one facing you) and he grabs on: W and S climb up and down, A and D go sideways, diagonals work, Space is a climb jump the way you hold (it costs a chunk of the green bar under the middle of the screen), Ctrl lets go. At the top he climbs onto it by himself. Its other faces are 70 and 55 degrees, and the back is a 40 degree slope you can walk down. The bar lasts 5 s climbing up at Climbing level 1 (hanging still drains a fifth as fast, sideways 2/3, down half); empty, he falls (no fall damage) and can't grab again until it has filled up on the ground. To the right of the start, past the knights, are two wooden fences (1 m and 1.35 m) and a stone block: run at them and press Space (or jump into them) to vault over a fence or climb onto the block. Ladders work exactly as before. Climbing only works on hills and rock, never buildings, trees or fences. Test switches: `-ClimbKeys=<up>_<right>_<from>_<for>` (hundredths), `-ClimbDo=jump<at>`, `letgo<at>`, `space<at>`; logs JEEBCLIMB, JEEBMANTLE, JEEBCLIMBPOSE.

**The test room's extras (2026-11-06, night):** walk round the weapon board. On its back is a new board, "EVERYTHING ELSE": potions, rings, amulets, the helm brush, logs, ores, gems, fish and animal drops; look at one and press E for a copy. Past the board's right end stands a tower with a ladder on the side facing you (walk into the ladder to climb, W and S on it). Past its left end is a pond (fishing rod in hand, walk up and click). Trees, rocks and fish never run out for now (`UnlimitedNodes` in `Config/DefaultGame.ini`). The axe and pickaxe now fight like the sword when they are not on a tree or rock: the two swings, the charge, the kick, the low sweep and the dash. The sword's two swings are final, so T is gone. The axe swing is a full-body clip; click at a tree or rock and he walks the last bit by himself so the steel meets it. You can't change the potion slot while a potion is cooling (about 10 s for testing). Test switches: `-GatherFrom=<distance>_<sideways>`, `-ShotEvery=1` (a picture every frame), `-ShieldArmTune=ArmX_ArmY_ForeX_ForeY_ForeZ_Out`, `-LabAt=<x>_<y>` (starts him there in MoveLab: tower ladder 650_1650), `-SuitClimbUp` (keeps climbing), `-SuitDo=fishnode`, `-ShotUnderRed` (the mail under the armour shows red, to check it never comes through a plate; it should only show in real gaps); logs JEEBCONTACT, JEEBHANDS, JEEBREACH and JEEBDRINK.

**The newest batch (2026-11-07):** one click on a tree or rock now means about 10 swings (`GatherSwings` in `Content/Data/Tuning.ini` [Gather]); walking away, jumping, rolling, a stun, a knockdown or changing weapon stops him sooner. If the pack is full he keeps swinging but wins nothing and a red message says so, and the rock or tree keeps its charge, so sell or drop something to get ore. The shield goes on his back while fishing. The fishing rod and the big two-handed sword swing like the sword (the two swings, the charge, the low sweep, the dash, the kick); the big sword holds both hands on the grip and has its own two cuts. The sword's second swing is a flat cut from high on his left to a little lower on his right. The white trail on the low sweep starts at `SweepTrailFrom` in Tuning.ini [Sweep] (a share of the sweep, no build needed: bigger means a later start). Sprint is 15% slower (`SprintSpeed`). Weapons that can't take a shield grey out the shield slot on the bottom bar. With the cursor out (Tab), the left and right buttons do nothing in the world: no shot, no charge, no zoom. The weapon, armour and everything-else boards in MoveLab are lower. Test films with `-ShotSeconds=` now run their full length (they used to be cut off 11 s after the level started); log JEEBGATHER follows the work.

**Trying the new things (2026-11-06):** in MoveLab a tree stands 8 m to your left and a rock 8 m to your right. Take an axe in hand, walk up to the tree, face it and click; the same with a pickaxe on the rock. Press **Q** to drink (it takes about 2 s at half speed, and a roll or a blow stops it without using the potion). With the cursor out (**Tab**), click anything on the bottom bar (weapon, shield, arrows, potion) and a small window opens right above it with what you could swap in. Clicking outside or the same slot again closes it. Keys 1, 2, R, F and Q still work as before. The new longbow and crossbow are on the armour wall, in the store and at the smith; test pictures `-ShotBowKind=21` (longbow) or `22` (crossbow), `-ShotArrow=<tier 0-5>`. `-ShotOff=<digits>` takes armour off (0 helmet, 1 chest, 2 shoulders, 3 gauntlets, 4 boots; `-ShotOff=12` takes two). `-ShieldRestTune=Share_Out_Turn_Tilt`, `-FlaskAt=X_Y_Z` and `-HandFit=X_Y_Z_Pitch_Yaw_Roll` try other fits without a rebuild.

**Sword smash attack:** hold the left mouse button (a quick or slow click is still the normal jab). After about a quarter of a second the knight stops at hit 1's pull-back and charges, walking at half speed and trembling more as it builds. It is full after 1 s, stays full for 2 s more, then goes by itself. Let go any time: hit 1's swipe, from about 1x (uncharged) to 2.5x a normal sword hit (full). After the swipe he always pauses for the same moment (end lag): he can't move, block, roll or jump. A click in the last moment of the pause comes out as soon as it ends. Try a full charge on the sparring knight, then an early release, and compare the numbers.

**Shields:** a raised shield guards a thin curved band in front of the knight, drawn see-through blue, about as far out as the shield itself (60-80 cm from his centre), reaching 45 degrees to either side of straight ahead. From the front it is an arch: straight sides from his feet to his belt, rounding up to the top of his helm in the middle (lower when he crouches). Whatever your sword's red boxes meet first along their path wins: the wedge first is a block; his body first (from the side or behind, the blade reaching his yellow boxes before it crosses the wedge) is a clean hit that lands in full, with no bounce and no end lag, even if the blade goes on through the wedge afterwards. A blocked blow never hurts him: the shield's health takes it, one for one (the blue number). The blow that empties it breaks it: his shield drops and stays down until it has healed to full (3 s after your last hit here; 6 s for the pen knight). Yours works the same way, and holding yours up costs 25 health a second. Hitting a shield bounces your sword back: the swing is over, you are knocked back a step and stuck (no moving, swinging, blocking, rolling or jumping) for 0.405 s after a jab hit, 0.81 s after a smash attack or a sweep. A click right at the end comes out as soon as you can move. A gold kite shield has 200 health; the numbers are in `Tiers.csv` (ShieldHealth), `ShieldStyles.csv` and `Tuning.ini` [Guard].

**Kick (scroll the wheel up):** rock, paper, scissors: the sword is stopped by a raised shield, the kick goes through it. The knight turns his whole body to his left and drives his right leg straight out at the target, level at hip height, toes turned to his left (like Ken's forward smash kick), fists up; it comes out fast and its hit box is just ahead of the sole; then the leg folds back and sets down. From the moment the kick starts until the leg is down he can't move, turn, roll or jump (end lag). It does a little damage and knocks anyone it hits down onto his back, shield or not; on a raised shield it also crushes half of what its health has left. But attack beats kick: kick into a knight whose sword is coming (a jab, smash or sweep) and YOU fall instead (and a blow that lands while you kick knocks you down). The sparring knights get up a different way each time, never the same way twice running (straight up, or rolling to their left or right), and walk back to their spots. Knocked down yourself (by another player's kick in MoveLab): once you land, A or D rolls you away that way, and W, S, Space or a click gets you straight up; after 1.5 s on the ground you get up by yourself, and getting up or rolling away can't be hit for a moment. Strafe mode is now on from the start (wheel click or X still turns it off).

**Weapon bar:** keys 1 to 5 take the weapon in that slot (the number shows in each slot's corner); R steps to the next one, left to right, round to the first again (empty slots are skipped). With the cursor out (Tab), clicking a slot takes it in hand too. The mouse wheel no longer changes weapons (wheel up kicks).

**Low sweep (scroll the wheel down, with a sword, axe or pickaxe and the like):** like Link's down tilt: he drops into a squat and sweeps the blade flat along the ground in front of him, right to left (out in about 0.13 s, rooted until it is over, 0.55 s). It reaches a knight lying on the ground: kick him down, read which way he will get up (straight up, or a roll to either side), and sweep there. Whoever it hits is launched about a metre up and a little back, and lands on his back again, so the read starts over. Launched yourself, hold a direction (W A S D) to drift that way (DI, as in Smash). Try it on the shielded knight: kick, then sweep before he gets up. A kick that lands has much less end lag, and the sweep can cut straight into it: wheel up, then wheel down as soon as the foot connects (or already during the kick: the wheel-down waits and comes out the moment it lands) and the blade catches him as he falls.

**Dash attack (sprint forward, then click, sword in hand):** a lunge: the hand draws back by his ribs and stabs the blade straight out ahead at full reach like a spear, the right foot thrust forward and the left leg stretched out behind, sliding on with the momentum. It is out in about 0.11 s and hits like a smash attack charged 20% (damage, hitstun and push), with a smash attack's end lag; on a raised shield it bounces off like a smash. Rooted until it is over.

**Bow (slot 2 of the starting kit; with 99 iron and 99 fire arrows):** with the bow in hand the shield goes away and a quiver of arrows shows on his back. Hold the **left or right** button to draw: he turns side-on, the bow up in his left hand and the string pulled back toward his face, walking at half speed while the camera closes in over his right shoulder and a reticle shows where the arrow will go (the ring closes as the draw fills and turns gold when full). The draw fills in 1 s and stays full for as long as you hold it. Let go to shoot; with the other button still down he keeps drawing and shoots when that one is let go too. The crossbow works like a sniper rifle: hold the right button to bring it up with the camera in close, and click the left button to shoot (no draw to wait for). Letting go of the right button lowers it without shooting; the left button alone does nothing. You can sprint while it reloads. The fuller the draw, the faster (so further, with less drop) and harder the arrow flies. Switching weapons, rolling, kicking, being hit or knocked down lets the string down without shooting (the arrow is kept). The kick (wheel up) still works with the bow. The arrow slot, right of the shield slot, shows which arrows you shoot next and how many are left: F (or a click on it with Tab) picks the next kind. Fire arrows glow, light up what is near them, and keep burning what they hit for a few seconds (3 x 2 damage).

**Arrows where they land:** an arrow sticks wherever it lands for 2 minutes: in the ground, a tree trunk, a wall, a knight's armour, or the shield itself (it moves with the shield). One that only touches the blue range of a raised shield (not the shield) glances off to that side and drops into the grass. Arrows lying in the world can be picked up again with E ("Press E to pick up the arrow"); arrows that struck a knight's armour or shield (and ones that glanced off a shield) are spent: they are never offered, so nobody is asked mid-fight. A shield takes an arrow's damage out of its health, one for one, and the archer never bounces back. In MoveLab arrows hit other players too (PvP testing).

**Hitstun:** a clean sword hit stops whatever the knight was doing; he can't move, swing, block, roll or jump for a moment (0.45 s after jab hit 1, so hit 2 always follows; 0.32 s after hit 2, which pushes him clear; 0.5 to 0.8 s after a smash). Your attack beats his if it lands first. The pen knight's cut stuns you for 0.35 s, and he now reaches 2 m (was 2.4 m, further than your sword).
