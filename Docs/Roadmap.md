# Jeebsland Roadmap (design ideas, no code yet)

Running lists from design conversations. Inspirations: Skyrim, Harry Potter, Lord of the Rings, Red Rising, Hunger Games, RuneScape, Zelda.
Cost tags: [S] small (fits current procedural style), [M] moderate, [L] big new system or art.

## Decided (2026-10-12 design chat)
- The sun stays as it is; it is a visual choice with no lore behind it.
- Blackrock is an ordinary walled town. No big main plot: a loose theme, many side quests, each a small self-contained story with its own NPC arc. Feuds between towns come from side-quest characters.
- Many knights, no hero: quest givers have their own arcs and the player is a helper.
- Every town has one **Trial**, central to the town; all of that town's side quests feed into it. Trials are OPTIONAL prestige (never required to progress).
- Every side quest ends with a **rare variant** of a normal item (e.g. a bronze sword averaging +3 damage). Same model with a tint or trim, so the art cost is tiny. [S]
- An **overall rank**, separate from skill levels, is visible to everyone on the character. The Red Helm Brush could be the first rung (rank colour on helm brush or cape). [M]
- Names come from CLO managers with a medieval feel.
- Future classes: wizards and trolls (own weapons, moves, looks). Big builds [L]; plan towns first so each class has a home. Trolls: playable, huge hitbox, would need camera, doorway and ladder rework.

## Towns and Trials (first expansion: four towns)
- **Blackrock: The Ladder.** Ranked arena, Pit > Wall > Gate > Keep; each rung a duel or wave fight. PvE until PvP exists, then ranked PvP. Rank banner in town.
- **Oak Hill (forest): The Hunt.** Timed Hunger Games-style trial; a group is released into a zone with a few rules; last one standing or best haul wins.
- **Steele Creek (marsh): The Wade.** Endurance course of hazards (the poison smoke cloud is the obstacle); no PvP, race the clock.
- **Silver Point (mountain): The Climb.** Vertical course using the ladder and climbing systems.
- **Carlyle (scholar town): The Examination.** Where wizards come from; puzzle or spell test.
- Backup names: Birch Grove, Deerpath (forest); Anchorage (marsh); Stone Point, Highbridge (mountain); Barings, Palmer Square (scholar).
