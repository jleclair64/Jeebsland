## Improved

- **Townsfolk are real characters.** Every villager, shopkeeper, banker, guard and quest giver is one of six animated Meshy characters instead of boxes and spheres; trolls use the smith's body with green-grey skin. `jeeb.Npc.Models 0` shows the old shapes for comparison.
- **New staff and wand models**, with proper casting moves, a glowing orb and crystal, and a glowing bolt. The staff is carried upright and lies across the back when stowed.
- **Forest trees** get their chop node only when a player is near (5,610 fewer actors at start).
- **Spawning in Oak Hill:** new players and deaths wake on 22 spread spots by the plaza oak, so players don't stack.

## How it was checked

- Each piece was tested by the agent that built it, the online ones with a real server and client.
- All five reviewers then ran on the combined build:
  - **Package: passed.** Every new asset and data file is in the package; no new test switch is left on.
  - **Performance: passed.** Nothing costs more than about 0.3 ms on any view measured. The new townsfolk are cheaper than the old boxes. Steele Creek costs the same as Oak Hill.
  - **Online: failed on one serious bug, now fixed.** Clients crashed on joining a world with the new townsfolk. After the fix, a server and two clients ran the quest, bandit (23 checks), emote and travel tests together, and every world hash matched.
  - **Looks and movement: failed on a list of problems, now fixed** (floating name tags, the staff through the legs when casting, a sword across the thighs in the bow emote, villagers walking sideways at corners, forts too small, Steele Creek too bare, and more).
- A second, narrower check of the fixes on the final build passed online and found some look and movement problems that are still open: see "Second check" at the end.

## Second check (on the final build)

Three reviewers looked again, only at what the fix round changed. This was the second and last round, so what they found is listed here for you instead of being fixed again. Pictures of the failures are in `Saved/Review/`.

- **Online: passed.** Both clients join with the new townsfolk and stay up; every world hash matches on server and both clients; bandits 23 of 23, quests 5 of 5, travel 8 of 8, an emote seen by the other player.
- **The "bridge" corrections are not the bridge.** The same small position corrections appear when a client starts running from a standstill on flat ground. It is a movement-start mismatch in the knight's movement, or an artefact of the scripted test. Nobody compared it with yesterday's build, so I can't say whether today's work caused it. No corrections happen while running steadily.
- **Still failing, looks:**
  - The staff's orb glows white, not cyan, and blows out at night (`staff_glow.png`). The bolt's light is also large, about 3 m wide at dusk. Both are strength numbers in `Tools/fixlook_assets.py`.
  - Trolls read as green-painted men (`trollA.png`): the skin is a pale mint, and at 1.18x they look man-sized. A darker, greyer olive and about 1.3x in `Content/Data/PeopleWho.csv` is the suggested change; really they need a model of their own.
  - The north fort has two trees inside its walls since it was enlarged (`fortair.png`). The tree clearing still uses the old size.
  - Steele Creek still reads as a meadow village (`camp_steele.png`): 38 small trees are not enough to feel like forest.
- **Still failing, movement:**
  - The staff snaps from a flat hold to overhead in the first 0.13 s of its cast, and in the upright carry the fist sits 5 to 10 cm off the shaft with the other arm held stiffly (`stR_close.png`, `stF_close.png`).
  - A bandit detouring round an obstacle keeps facing its target while stepping 70 to 110 degrees off, so it crab-walks. Seen in logs, not in pictures.
- **Passed:** the wand cast and its glow, the staff carried upright and stowed, emote gear timing, name tags and quest marks at head height with the E prompt beside the name, villager colours on the plaza, the camp flame, the bridge's stone colour, the emote wheel, villagers walking without foot sliding.
- **Nobody managed to check:** run and sprint with the staff, the salute's hand at the visor, an emote cancelled by attacking, the villager head-turn as you pass behind, a troll's name tag (none drew in the one test), the banker's robe, bandit chase, swing and fall, Blackrock's villagers, and the quest-complete title. The reviewers' test tools got in the way: the walk test stops the knight after under a second, and the picture cropping assumes a larger frame than the off-screen runs produce.
